## Architecture trade-study criteria
Road and flight regulatory feasibility; mass and useful load; energy and propulsion complexity; takeoff/landing environment; stability and failure modes; transition complexity; maintainability; noise; cost; and required evidence.

Do not publish numeric performance targets until mission assumptions, architecture, and calculations have been reviewed.
