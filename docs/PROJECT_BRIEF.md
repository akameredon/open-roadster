# OPEN ROADSTER — Project Brief v1.0

## Mission
Develop an openly documented investigation into a two-seat roadable aircraft: a vehicle intended to operate on roads and in flight.

## Proposed distinction
An auditable public development process: transparent requirements, architecture decisions, research, reviews, and test evidence. We do not claim to be the first open-source aircraft or first flying-car project.

## Initial scope
- Two-seat roadable-aircraft concept
- Public engineering research and decision records
- Concept visualization clearly labeled
- Requirements and regulatory research
- Architecture comparison before design selection
- Feasibility assessment before physical prototyping

## Out of scope
- Claims of flight capability, range, speed, payload, safety, road legality, or certification
- Vehicle sales or paid reservations
- Crewed flight tests as an early milestone
- Construction instructions for an unvalidated passenger aircraft

## Stakeholders
Aerospace, mechanical, electrical, controls, automotive, software, human-factors, safety, regulatory, industrial-design, manufacturing specialists, universities, potential users, and open-source contributors.

## Decision principles
1. Safety and regulatory feasibility are gates.
2. Evidence and traceability outrank aesthetic preference.
3. Record assumptions and revisit them.
4. Simulation is not physical validation.
5. A demonstrator requires qualified review, a safety case, budget, and test plan.

## First deliverables
1. Requirements and assumptions register
2. Regulatory pathway assessment for intended jurisdictions
3. Architecture trade study
4. Preliminary mass and energy budget
5. Hazard analysis and verification plan
6. Independent feasibility review
