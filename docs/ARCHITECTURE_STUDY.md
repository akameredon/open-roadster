# Architecture Study — Open Research

**Decision status: no architecture selected.**

## Candidate A — Fixed-wing roadable aircraft
Investigate a road vehicle with folding or deployable wings. Assess wing-borne cruise, ground footprint, deployment interlocks, and need for suitable takeoff/landing areas.

## Candidate B — Lift-plus-cruise eVTOL
Investigate separate vertical-lift and forward-flight systems. Assess added mass, energy demand, failure cases, noise, and practical roadability.

## Candidate C — Tilt-propulsion configuration
Investigate propulsion that changes orientation between vertical and forward flight. Assess transition control, mechanical complexity, redundancy, and failure behavior.

## Required output
For each candidate record assumptions, mission, benefits and disadvantages, mass/energy implications, regulatory questions, hazards, required expertise, validation needs, and reasons to continue or reject.

## Decision rule
Do not select based on visual appeal, popularity, or a single simulation. Require documented criteria and qualified aerospace and road-vehicle review. This is a research brief, not a construction guide.
