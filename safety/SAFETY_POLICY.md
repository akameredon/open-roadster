# Safety and Evidence Policy

OPEN ROADSTER is concept-stage research. Repository content is not a certified design, approved maintenance instruction, or authorization to build or operate an aircraft.

## Evidence labels
- **CONCEPT:** illustration or early idea; not validated.
- **RESEARCH:** sourced investigation with assumptions.
- **ANALYSIS:** calculation or simulation with methods and limitations.
- **REVIEWED:** reviewed by named people within stated scope; not automatically validated.
- **TESTED:** physical test with documented procedure, conditions, instrumentation, results, deviations, and limitations.
- **CERTIFIED/APPROVED:** use only when the relevant authority has formally granted approval and scope is stated.

## Safeguards
Maintain a hazard log and verification plan; use qualified specialists for safety-critical work; do not use passenger flight tests as an early milestone; do not treat AI images or unvalidated simulations as proof; operate tests only within applicable law and competent oversight; stop work when risk is unacceptable.

## Public communication
Label proposed-vehicle renders as concepts. Do not imply flight capability, legal road use, two-person payload capability, performance, or certification without appropriate evidence.
