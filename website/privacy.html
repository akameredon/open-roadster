PRIVACY NOTICE

How we handle waitlist data

Last updated: 18 October 2026. OPEN ROADSTER is a concept-stage, open-source engineering project. This page explains what the waitlist form collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Email address (required).
  • Full name and area of interest (both optional).
  • Consent record: the time you agreed and the version of the consent wording you saw.
  • Status records: timestamps for signup, email confirmation and unsubscribe, how many confirmation emails we sent you, and the signup source (for example, "website").
  • Security values: one-way hashes of confirmation and unsubscribe tokens. We do not store the raw confirmation token.

We do not ask for phone numbers, payment details, identity documents, or any other personal data.

Why we collect it

Only to send you OPEN ROADSTER project updates, which you agreed to by ticking the consent box, and to confirm and manage your subscription. We do not sell or rent your data and we do not share it for advertising.

How signup works

We use double opt-in. After you submit the form we email a confirmation link, which expires after 24 hours. Your address is treated as a confirmed subscriber only after you click it. If you did not request the email, ignore it and nothing happens. Unconfirmed signups are deleted after 30 days.

Where the data is stored

Subscriber data is kept in a private Google Sheet owned by the project maintainer, and emails are sent using Google's services (Google Apps Script and Gmail). Google therefore processes this data on our behalf, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The sheet is not shared publicly. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may collect technical information about visitors under its own privacy policy. The waitlist form does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How long we keep it

  • Confirmed subscribers: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data.
  • Unconfirmed signups: deleted after 30 days.
  • Unsubscribed addresses: we keep the email address and status so that we do not add you again by mistake. You can ask us to erase this record too.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe: every update email has an unsubscribe link. No account or login is needed.
  • Access or deletion: email us at macdonaldakadonye@gmail.com, or open an issue at the project's GitHub issues without including personal details, and we will arrange it privately. Depending on where you live, you may have additional legal rights over your data.

Project status

OPEN ROADSTER is a concept-stage research initiative. Signing up does not reserve a product, make a purchase, or create any commitment on either side.

This notice may change as the project grows. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
