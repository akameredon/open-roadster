# OPEN ROADSTER is recruiting its founding engineering community

What would it take to build a two-seat vehicle that can travel on roads and fly?

We're starting OPEN ROADSTER, an open engineering initiative investigating that challenge in public. The goal is to build a community where requirements, research, trade-offs, and decisions are visible — and where claims are matched to evidence.

We're looking for people with experience or a strong research interest in:
- Aerospace and mechanical engineering
- Automotive engineering and road-vehicle safety
- Flight controls, electrical systems, propulsion, and energy analysis
- Human factors, aviation regulation, and certification
- CAD, simulation, documentation, web development, and open-source community building

The project is at concept stage. There is no completed or flight-tested aircraft, and we make no performance, roadworthiness, or certification claims. Early contributions focus on research, requirements, architecture comparisons, and independent review — not building or flying an unvalidated vehicle.

Start here: https://github.com/akameredon/open-roadster

Explore the open issues, introduce your relevant experience, and choose a research question you can help answer. Please keep sensitive personal details out of public issues.
