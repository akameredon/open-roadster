# Community Governance — Draft

## Roles
- Maintainers: repository health, documentation, moderation, process.
- Domain reviewers: assess work within their competence and disclose scope.
- Contributors: propose research, documentation, design, software, and analysis.
- Safety lead (to be appointed): coordinates hazard tracking; does not replace qualified engineering authorities.

## Decisions
Routine documentation decisions may be made by maintainers. Technical decisions require written rationale and alternatives. Safety-critical decisions require qualified domain review and evidence appropriate to risk. Community votes cannot waive legal requirements or verification.

## Transparency
Record date, context, options, assumptions, evidence, reviewers, unresolved risks, and conditions for revisiting a decision.

## Conduct
Treat people respectfully, criticize ideas rather than people, disclose conflicts of interest, and do not present speculative work as validated capability.

## Draft status
Review with contributors and legal counsel before formal adoption.
