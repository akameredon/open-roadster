# Organic Launch Plan — First 30 Days

Targets below are planning targets, not forecasts or guarantees.

## Week 1: credibility
Publish the brief, requirements, governance draft, and safety policy. Open issues for regulatory research, architecture comparison, and contributor onboarding.

## Week 2: visual launch
Publish one concept render and a 15–30 second reveal. Share on LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and relevant engineering communities. Label renders as conceptual and link to the repository and waitlist.

## Week 3: contributor recruitment
Publish an architecture question with a structured comparison template. Invite qualified aerospace, automotive, safety, and regulatory specialists and relevant university groups.

## Week 4: evidence update
Publish findings, unresolved questions, and next steps. Review verified signups, conversion, and meaningful contributor activity; adjust based on evidence rather than likes.

## Initial targets
- 1,000 qualified landing-page visits
- 100–200 verified subscribers
- 5–10 meaningful contributors
- Four public updates
- Five useful technical issue discussions
