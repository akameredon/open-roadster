# Contributing to OPEN ROADSTER

Contributions may include literature reviews, regulatory research, requirements analysis, CAD documentation, simulation methods, accessibility, website work, and project operations.

## Before contributing
1. Read the project brief and safety policy.
2. Search existing issues first.
3. State the question, assumptions, sources, uncertainty, and intended use.
4. Keep concept art separate from engineering evidence.
5. Do not publish confidential, export-controlled, or improperly licensed material.

## Pull requests
Keep changes focused; include sources and reproducible steps; explain limitations and what has not been validated; avoid unsupported safety, performance, certification, or readiness claims.

## Safety-critical work
Changes affecting structures, propulsion, controls, batteries, flight dynamics, road safety, or test procedures require qualified review. Public discussion and maintainer approval do not validate such work.

## Licensing
Licensing is being established by content category. Identify third-party material and agree to documented contribution terms before submitting copyrightable design work.
